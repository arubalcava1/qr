# Privacy Policy — WiFi QR

_Last updated: 2026-08-18_

WiFi QR is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains exactly what the extension does and does not do with your data.

## What the extension stores

When you generate a QR code, the extension may save the following to your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`):

- The network name (SSID) you entered
- The network password you entered
- The time it was generated

This is used solely to populate the "recent networks" list in the popup, so you don't have to retype a network you've already generated a code for.

## What the extension does NOT do

- It does not send any data to a server. There is no backend — the extension has no network permissions and makes no network requests.
- It does not use analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
- It does not sync your data to your Google account or any other service.
- It does not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party, for any purpose.
- It does not use or inject remote code — everything the extension runs ships inside the extension package.

## Where your data lives

Everything is stored locally in Chrome's extension storage, scoped to your browser profile on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere.

## Deleting your data

- Remove a single saved network with the **×** next to it in the history panel, or **Clear all** to remove everything.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data automatically.

## Permissions

The extension requests one permission:

- **`storage`** — used only for the recent-networks feature described above.
